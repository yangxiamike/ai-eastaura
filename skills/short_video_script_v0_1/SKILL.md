# short_video_script v0.1

## Purpose

Generate deterministic English short-video scripts for Eastaura content assets.

## Structure

1. Hook: name a relatable stress, sleep, energy, or reset tension.
2. Context: explain the Eastaura retreat angle in plain language.
3. Boundary: state that clinical care belongs with licensed providers and human review.
4. Value: show a calm, structured China-based wellness journey.
5. CTA: invite the viewer to complete intake before recommendations.

## Guardrails

- No cure, heal, treat, guarantee, reverse disease, or doctor-approved claims.
- Do not diagnose viewers.
- Do not prescribe herbs, acupuncture, medication, or treatment plans.
- Keep the tone calm, grounded, and specific.
