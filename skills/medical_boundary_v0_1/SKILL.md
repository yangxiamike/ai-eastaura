# medical_boundary v0.1

## 适用场景

检查官网文案、邮件、Proposal、短视频脚本和 AI 输出是否越过医疗广告或诊疗边界。

## 允许方向

- wellness experience
- stress recovery support
- sleep routine reset
- relaxation and cultural learning
- licensed providers handle clinical services where relevant

## 禁止方向

- 承诺疗效
- 诊断疾病
- 替代医生
- 暗示一定治愈
- 对针灸、中药、处方或医疗服务做未经资质支持的具体承诺

## 安全改写原则

把 treatment/cure 改成 support/explore/help guests understand。把 guaranteed results 改成 personalized experience with clear safety boundaries。
