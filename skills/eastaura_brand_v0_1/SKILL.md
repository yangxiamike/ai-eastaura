# eastaura_brand v0.1

## 适用场景

所有面向海外用户的官网文案、邮件、Lead 摘要、Proposal 草稿和内容脚本。

## 品牌定位

Eastaura is a China-based TCM wellness retreat for stress recovery, sleep reset, and whole-person balance.

## 推荐表达

- stress recovery
- sleep reset
- whole-person balance
- TCM wellness retreat
- concierge-style wellness journey
- transparent safety boundaries
- guided by licensed providers where clinical care is involved

## 禁止表达

- cure
- guaranteed result
- reverse disease
- medical miracle
- replace your doctor
- diagnose
- treatment plan without licensed clinician review

## 语气

清楚、温和、可信、边界明确。避免神秘化中医，避免把文化体验包装成医疗承诺。

## 人工审核规则

涉及疾病、治疗、处方、针灸、中药、严重心理问题、孕期、术后恢复或慢性病管理时，必须进入人工审核。
