# visual_prompt v0.1

## Purpose

Draft safe visual prompts for Eastaura content assets and storyboard scenes.

## Style

- boutique wellness retreat
- warm natural light
- calm China-based setting
- editorial, human, understated luxury
- realistic guest experience

## Avoid

- before-after medical transformation
- hospital urgency
- exaggerated pain or disease imagery
- cultural stereotypes
- fake doctor endorsements

## Human Review

Any prompt showing clinical consultation, acupuncture, herbs, prescriptions, or patient claims requires human review before publishing.
