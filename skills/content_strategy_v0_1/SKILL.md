# content_strategy v0.1

## Purpose

Create Eastaura content angles for overseas wellness audiences without medical promises.

## Inputs

- campaign objective
- target audience
- channel
- content format
- offer or landing page

## Rules

- Lead with stress recovery, sleep routine reset, cultural learning, and concierge safety.
- Keep claims educational and experiential.
- Route disease, treatment, prescription, acupuncture, herbs, pregnancy, surgery, and chronic-condition claims to human review.
- Do not imply guaranteed outcomes or clinical advice.

## Output

- English title
- short brief
- channel angle
- suggested CTA
- compliance notes
