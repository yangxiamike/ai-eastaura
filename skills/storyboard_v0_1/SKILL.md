# storyboard v0.1

## Purpose

Turn an approved short-video draft into 4-5 human-reviewable scenes.

## Scene Requirements

- `id`
- `order`
- `title`
- `visual`
- optional `narration`
- optional `durationSeconds`

## Default Arc

1. Quiet arrival or problem framing.
2. Intake and concierge context.
3. TCM wellness boundary with licensed-provider framing.
4. Retreat rhythm and sensory proof.
5. Intake CTA.

## Guardrails

- Visuals may show wellness context, not guaranteed outcomes.
- Medical-looking scenes must be contextual and reviewed by a human.
