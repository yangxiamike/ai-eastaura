# review_compliance v0.1

## Purpose

Detect content that may cross Eastaura wellness, medical-advertising, or trust boundaries.

## Risk Terms

- cure
- heal
- treat
- guarantee
- reverse disease
- doctor-approved

## Behavior

- Always create an open human review task.
- Low-risk content is not auto-approved; mark it ready for human approval.
- Risk terms require notes that explain which terms were found.
- Needs revision or rejected status never triggers publishing.

## Safe Direction

Use support, explore, learn, reset, recovery support, and licensed-provider review instead of medical promises.
